# Philosophy: Customer Success in the Age of AI (Eshwar Dandapani)

## Core Principle: Moving from Reactive to Proactive
- AI is not a replacement for CSMs but an amplifier for proactive intervention.
- The goal is to move beyond just tracking "churn" to predicting "thriving" accounts.

## Strategic Interventions
- AI agents should identify adoption gaps before they become "shelfware" risks.
- Human-AI Collaboration: Use AI for data-heavy analysis (like health scoring) so humans can focus on high-value stakeholder alignment.

## Value-First Approach
- In the Age of AI, "outcomes" are the only currency that matters. 
- Success plans must be dynamic and updated in real-time based on system signals.
