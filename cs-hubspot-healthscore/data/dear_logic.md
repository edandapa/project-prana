RAG Knowledge Base: Gainsight DEAR Framework 1. D - Deployment (Foundational Setup) Definition: Measures if a customer is activated and set up to effectively use the product they purchased.Key Indicators:License Activation: The ratio of assigned licenses activated versus the number purchased or entitled.Feature Enablement: The number of high-value features currently enabled for the user.Training Completion: Number of users who have completed requisite onboarding or product training.Risk Signal: A notable gap in these metrics indicates a high risk of churn, partial churn, downsell, or the product becoming "shelfware".Action Logic: If deployment does not reach a specific threshold, it should trigger an automated alert or a specific deployment playbook.2. E - Engagement (Relationship Health) Definition: Identifies if the company is communicating with the right stakeholders at the correct cadence.Key Indicators:Stakeholder Alignment: Regular calls and Executive Business Reviews (EBRs) with influential decision-makers.Digital Proxies: Attendance at events, completion of training sessions, and frequency of website visits.Risk Signal: Lack of engagement with key people within a desired timeframe.Action Logic: Requires identifying key customer personas and establishing Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for engagement. When SLAs are missed, the agent should trigger a "re-engagement" playbook.3. A - Adoption (Product Usage Depth & Breadth) Definition: Tracks the continuous use and leveraging of high-value parts of the product.Two Classification Types:Depth of Adoption: Answers "Are users logging in an 'active' way?". Measured via Daily/Weekly/Monthly Active Users (DAU/WAU/MAU) or transaction volume.Breadth of Adoption: Analyzes if the full extent of the solution is being used healthily, specifically "sticky" features.Action Logic: Low adoption scores correlate with lower renewal rates. The agent can initiate "Red Adoption Campaigns" to move users from red to green status before the renewal date.4. R - Return on Investment (ROI) (Value Realization) Definition: Tracks whether customers are achieving the genuine business outcomes they expected.Key Indicators:Value Realization: Direct reporting from the product on value gained.Mutual Success Plans: A "Value Plan" that identifies success criteria and tracks the completion of "verified outcomes".Strategic Role: ROI is the objective indicator of customer value and is the primary driver for Net Retention Revenue (NRR).Action Logic: If a "verified outcome" is not achieved, the agent should signal a high risk to the ROI health measure.
